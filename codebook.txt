
CODEBOOK - Predicting NBA Regular Season Win Totals (2000–2026)
================================================================

Source: Basketball Reference (Sports Reference LLC), https://www.basketball-reference.com/
Tables: Team Per 100 Possessions (Team + Opponent), Team Advanced
Unit of observation: one team's single regular season
N = 805 team-seasons (27 seasons, 1999-00 through 2025-26)



IDENTIFIERS (kept, not modeled on)

season          int     Season end year (2016 = 2015-16 season).
team            chr     Full team name for that season.
era             factor  Derived: 2000-2007 / 2008-2014 / 2015-2026.


OUTCOME

wins            int     Regular season wins (0-82). Regression response.



CONTEXT PREDICTORS (both recipes)

age             num     Minutes-weighted average roster age.
pace            num     Possessions per 48 minutes.
sos             num     Strength of Schedule (avg opponent strength, pts).


MARGIN / DERIVED METRICS  (FULL recipe ONLY)

off_rtg         num     Offensive Rating: points scored per 100 poss.
def_rtg         num     Defensive Rating: points allowed per 100 poss.
net_rtg         num     off_rtg - def_rtg; per-possession point margin.
srs             num     Simple Rating System: point margin adj. for schedule.


PURE OFFENSE COMPONENTS (both recipes)

ts_pct          num     True Shooting % (efficiency incl. 3s and FTs).
efg_pct         num     Effective FG % (weights made 3s 1.5x).
fg3ar           num     3-point attempt rate (share of FGA from 3).
ftr             num     Free throw rate (FTA per FGA).
tov_pct         num     Turnover percentage (turnovers per 100 plays).
orb_pct         num     Offensive rebound percentage.
ast             num     Assists per 100 possessions.
stl             num     Steals per 100 possessions.
blk             num     Blocks per 100 possessions.


PURE DEFENSE COMPONENTS (both recipes)

opp_efg_pct     num     Opponent effective FG % allowed.
opp_tov_pct     num     Opponent turnover % forced.
opp_ft_rate     num     Opponent free throw rate allowed.
drb_pct 	num     Defensive rebound percentage.
